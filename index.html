Privacy-Preserving Shopping Intelligence

M.Sc. research project · 2026 — ongoing with Microsoft

Can an AI give you equally smart shopping suggestions while your browsing data stays on your own device? I built the same recommender three ways — cloud, federated learning and fully on-device — and measured quality against model size, latency, network traffic and cold-start behaviour. The work targets shopping assistance in Microsoft Edge.

Adversarially Hardened Hierarchical ML for IoT Intrusion Detection

Eid Abd Elrihem, Ahmed Sherif, Ahmed Abdelhamed · DOI 10.1109/ITC-Egypt70422.2026.11765029

Billions of IoT devices can't run normal security software. I built a guard that triages network traffic the way an analyst would — attack or not, which family, which exact attack — and stays accurate on rare attacks where flat classifiers collapse. Then I attacked it with three adversarial evasion methods to prove it holds.

Binary gate→7 attack families→33 sub-type specialists→SHAP-explained alert
98.52%accuracy on ~7M flows, 34 classes
0.941macro F1 vs. 0.714 in the original benchmark
~20µsper connection on an ordinary CPU
